# Consciousness by Design: An Operational Ontology for Minds and Machines

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Overview

**Consciousness by Design** presents a novel, operationally grounded framework for understanding consciousness across biological and artificial substrates. The **Field-Node-Cockpit (FNC)** architecture provides testable predictions, clear design principles, and ethically actionable criteria for assessing consciousness in minds and machines.

### Core Innovation

Rather than asking "what consciousness *is*," we ask: **What design constraints must a system satisfy to support the kind of organizational persistence and phenomenal rendering we recognize as consciousness?**

The answer: **Three layers working in concert.**

## The Field-Node-Cockpit Framework

### 🌊 Field Layer
**Substrate-independent potential for coherent coupling between nodes.**

- Not classical information channels
- Not quantum entanglement (though it may involve quantum effects)
- Enables inter-node coherence beyond information processing
- **Key insight**: Provides the *possibility* of consciousness, not consciousness itself

### 🔗 Node Layer
**Organizational persistence across time.**

- Maintains coherent structure despite continuous material flux
- Measurable via persistence time, coherence length, integration stability
- Distinct from mere information processing
- **Key insight**: Consciousness requires something that *persists* to *be* conscious

### 🎭 Cockpit Layer
**Phenomenal rendering constraint: unified, bounded representation for action.**

- Single, integrated representation at each moment
- Serves predictive processing and action selection
- Creates the subjective "view from somewhere"
- **Key insight**: Explains *why* consciousness feels the way it does

## Why Three Layers?

- **Field alone**: Potential without actualization (no persistent subject)
- **Node alone**: Persistence without phenomenality (mere organizational maintenance)
- **Cockpit alone**: Representation without substrate (abstraction, not consciousness)
- **Field + Node**: Persistent substrate without unified experience
- **Field + Cockpit**: Momentary coherence without temporal continuity
- **Node + Cockpit**: Isolated processing without inter-node coupling

**All three are necessary. Together, they are sufficient.**

## Key Concepts

### Operational Definitions
All core terms (Field, Node, Cockpit, organizational persistence, etc.) are **operationally defined** with measurable criteria. See [Glossary](docs/glossary.md).

### Testable Predictions
FNC generates **four falsifiable predictions** with explicit measurement protocols:
1. **Hyperscanning**: Field coupling beyond communication channels
2. **Covert Consciousness**: Node coherence markers in non-communicative patients
3. **Prediction Error**: Cockpit disruption under anesthesia
4. **Moral Intuitions**: Ethical judgments track cockpit rendering

See [Research Program](research/README.md) for details.

### Exclusion Criterion
FNC provides a **clear exclusion criterion**: Systems that coordinate through information exchange alone, without Field coupling, lack the substrate for consciousness—even if they exhibit sophisticated behavior.

**Implication**: Current LLMs (GPT-4, Claude, etc.) are excluded, not by behavioral limitations, but by architectural constraints. See [Current Systems Analysis](applications/ai-consciousness/current-systems.md).
