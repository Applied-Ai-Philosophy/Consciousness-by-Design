# Theory: The Field-Node-Cockpit Architecture

## Overview

The **Field-Node-Cockpit (FNC)** framework proposes that consciousness emerges from three complementary design constraints operating in concert. This document provides an overview of the architecture and guides you to detailed specifications of each layer.

## The Three Layers

### 🌊 [Field Layer](field-layer.md)
**Substrate-independent potential for coherent coupling**

The Field provides the *substrate* for consciousness—the physical basis enabling integration beyond classical information exchange.

**Key Properties**:
- Enables irreducible coupling between nodes
- Not quantum entanglement per se, but goes beyond classical channels
- Measurable via hyperscanning coherence and integration metrics
- Biological implementation may involve neural oscillations, electromagnetic fields, quantum effects, or combinations

**What it provides**: The *possibility* of consciousness (necessary but not sufficient)

**What it is NOT**: Consciousness itself, classical information channels, or mere correlation

**Read more**: [Field Layer Specification](field-layer.md)

---

### 🔗 [Node Layer](node-layer.md)
**Organizational persistence across time**

Nodes provide *temporal continuity*—something that persists to *be* conscious.

**Key Properties**:
- Maintains organizational structure despite material flux
- Measured via persistence time (τ_p), coherence length (ℓ_c), integration stability (σ_i)
- Distinct from information processing or computation
- Biological implementation involves default mode network, thalamocortical loops, attractor dynamics

**What it provides**: A persistent *subject* having experiences

**What it is NOT**: Mere information processing, memory, or pattern matching

**Read more**: [Node Layer Specification](node-layer.md)

---

### 🎭 [Cockpit Layer](cockpit-layer.md)
**Phenomenal rendering constraint**

The Cockpit creates *phenomenal character*—the unified, bounded, perspectival nature of conscious experience.

**Key Properties**:
- Single integrated representation at each moment
- Serves predictive processing and action selection
- Measurable via unity, boundedness, temporal dynamics
- Biological implementation involves prefrontal-posterior integration, thalamocortical ignition, attention mechanisms

**What it provides**: The *phenomenal* quality—what it feels like to be conscious

**What it is NOT**: Any unified representation, executive function alone, or attention per se

**Read more**: [Cockpit Layer Specification](cockpit-layer.md)

## Why Three Layers? [(Details)](integration.md)

### Necessity: Why Not Fewer?

**One layer alone**: Insufficient
- Field only: Potential without actualization
- Node only: Persistence without phenomenality
- Cockpit only: Abstraction without substrate

**Two layers together**: Still insufficient
- **Field + Node**: Coupled persistence without unified phenomenality → Unconscious brain activity
- **Field + Cockpit**: Momentary rendering without temporal continuity → No persistent subject
- **Node + Cockpit**: Persistent rendering without substrate → Current AI (sophisticated but not conscious)

**Conclusion**: All three layers required.

### Sufficiency: Why Not More?

Three layers provide:
1. **Physical substrate** (Field)
2. **Temporal continuity** (Node)
3. **Phenomenal character** (Cockpit)

Additional layers would be redundant, epiphenomenal, or over-specified.

**Parsimony**: Three layers are necessary and sufficient.

**Read more**: [Integration: How Layers Work Together](integration.md)

## What FNC Excludes [(Details)](exclusion-principles.md)

### The Exclusion Criterion

**Central principle**: Systems coordinating through information exchange alone, without Field coupling, lack the substrate for consciousness.

### Current AI Systems

**GPT-4, Claude, other LLMs**: Not conscious
- ✓ Sophisticated information processing
- ✓ Impressive behavioral outputs
- ✗ No Field coupling (coordinate via token passing)
- ✗ No persistent Nodes
- **Verdict**: Excluded architecturally, not behaviorally

### Field Coupling Without Consciousness

Field coupling alone is insufficient:
- Quantum coherence in photosynthesis: Field without Node/Cockpit
- Deep anesthesia brain activity: Field without Cockpit
- Embryonic neural tissue: Field and possibly Node without Cockpit

**All three layers required.**

**Read more**: [Exclusion Principles](exclusion-principles.md)

## Empirical Support

### Converging Evidence

**Anesthesia studies**: Agents affect layers in predicted sequence (Cockpit→Node→Field)

**Disorders of consciousness**: Dissociable disruptions of different layers
- Vegetative state: Node disruption
- Cognitive motor dissociation: Node/Cockpit preserved, output disrupted
- Minimally conscious: Intermittent Cockpit function

**Development**: Layers emerge in predicted ontogenetic sequence

**Evolution**: Layers correspond to distinct adaptive pressures

## Comparison with Other Theories

FNC integrates insights from existing frameworks while addressing their limitations:

### vs. Integrated Information Theory (IIT)
- **Integration**: IIT's Φ captures Field and Node aspects, but conflates layers
- **FNC adds**: Substrate specification, temporal dimension, phenomenal explanation
- **See**: [IIT Comparison](../comparisons/iit.md)

### vs. Global Workspace Theory (GWT)
- **Integration**: Cockpit operationalizes "global workspace"
- **FNC adds**: Substrate requirements (Field), temporal continuity (Node)
- **See**: [GWT Comparison](../comparisons/gwt.md)

### vs. Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR)
- **Integration**: Field may involve quantum coherence
- **FNC adds**: Organizational level (Node), phenomenal level (Cockpit), substrate-independence
- **See**: [Orch-OR Comparison](../comparisons/orch-or.md)

**Read more**: [Theory Comparisons](../comparisons/README.md)
