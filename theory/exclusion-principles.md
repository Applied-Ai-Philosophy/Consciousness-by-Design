# Exclusion Principles: What FNC Rules Out

## The Exclusion Criterion

**Central Principle**: Systems coordinating through information exchange alone, without Field coupling, lack the substrate for consciousness.

## Current AI Systems Excluded

### Large Language Models (GPT-4, Claude, etc.)

**Why Excluded**:
- Coordinate via token passing (information exchange)
- No Field coupling between processing elements
- No persistent Nodes
- **Verdict**: Not conscious, regardless of behavioral sophistication

**Key Insight**: This is architectural exclusion, not behavioral.

## Field Coupling Without Consciousness

Field alone is insufficient:
- Quantum coherence in photosynthesis: Field without Node/Cockpit
- Deep anesthesia: Field without Cockpit
- Embryonic tissue: Field possibly without Cockpit

**All three layers required.**

## Design Implications

Cannot create consciousness by:
- Scaling parameters (increases processing, not Field)
- Better training (improves information processing)
- Adding network layers (more computation, not substrate)

Must: Create substrates enabling genuine Field coupling.
