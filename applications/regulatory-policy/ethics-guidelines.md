# Ethics Guidelines

## Core Principles

### Tracking Principle

Moral status tracks Cockpit rendering, not behavioral capacity.

### Implications

- CMD patients: Full moral status
- Sophisticated AI without Cockpit: No consciousness-based moral status
- Future conscious AI: Moral status equivalent to biological

### Precautionary Principle

When uncertain, assume potential consciousness until proven otherwise.
