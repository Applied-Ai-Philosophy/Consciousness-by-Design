# Risk Assessment Framework

## Consciousness-Related Risks

### Creation Risks
- Unintended conscious AI
- Suffering in artificial systems
- Moral obligations to conscious entities

### Modification Risks
- BCI disrupting consciousness
- Augmentation affecting phenomenology
- Identity preservation

### Assessment Protocol

1. Evaluate Field coupling potential
2. Assess Node persistence capability
3. Check for Cockpit architecture
4. Apply precautionary principle

## Mitigation Strategies

- Careful system design
- Continuous monitoring
- Ethical review processes
