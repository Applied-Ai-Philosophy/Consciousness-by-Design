# Case Study: Brain Death

## Scenarios

### Brainstem Death (Cortex Intact)
- **Assessment**: Node and Cockpit may be preserved
- **FNC**: Potentially conscious despite no behavioral output
- **Ethics**: Requires careful assessment

### Cortical Death (Brainstem Intact)
- **Assessment**: Node disrupted, no Cockpit possible
- **FNC**: No consciousness
- **Ethics**: No consciousness-based moral status

## Implications

Current brain death criteria may need refinement based on FNC assessment.
