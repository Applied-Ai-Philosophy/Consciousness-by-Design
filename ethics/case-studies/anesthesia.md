# Case Study: Surgical Anesthesia

## Scenario

Patient under general anesthesia for surgery.

## FNC Assessment

- **Cockpit**: Disrupted (no prediction error processing)
- **Node**: Preserved (returns after agent clears)
- **Verdict**: Temporarily unconscious

## Ethical Implications

- **Suspended moral status** during unconsciousness
- No suffering or phenomenal experience
- Full moral status returns upon emergence

## Clinical Protocols

- Monitor Cockpit markers to prevent awareness
- Ensure sufficient depth
- Minimize unnecessary depth (faster recovery)
