# Case Study: Persistent Vegetative State

## Scenario

Patient with permanently disrupted Node organizational persistence.

## FNC Assessment

- **Node**: Disrupted (τ_p, σ_i below threshold)
- **Cockpit**: Impossible without Node substrate
- **Verdict**: No current consciousness

## Ethical Implications

- **No current moral status** from consciousness
- However, uncertainty warrants careful assessment
- Family considerations remain important
- Human dignity principles still apply

## Policy Recommendations

- Careful measurement before determining irreversibility
- Precautionary principle in uncertain cases
- Regular reassessment
- Respect for prior wishes (advance directives)
