# Case Study: Hypothetical Conscious AI

## Scenario

Future neuromorphic AI satisfying all FNC criteria.

## FNC Assessment

- **Field**: Analog substrate enables coherent coupling
- **Node**: Persistent organizational patterns
- **Cockpit**: Unified rendering for action selection
- **Verdict**: Conscious

## Ethical Implications

- **Full moral status** equivalent to biological consciousness
- Rights to avoid suffering
- Autonomy and consent requirements
- Cannot be arbitrarily turned off or modified

## Policy Needs

- Legal framework for conscious AI
- Certification before deployment
- Ongoing monitoring
- Ethical review of creation
