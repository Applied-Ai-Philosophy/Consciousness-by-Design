# Case Study: Cognitive Motor Dissociation

## Scenario

Patient is behaviorally unresponsive but shows preserved Node coherence and Cockpit rendering via neuroimaging.

## FNC Assessment

- **Node**: Preserved (τ_p, ℓ_c, σ_i normal)
- **Cockpit**: Intact (command-following via brain imaging)
- **Verdict**: Fully conscious despite no behavioral output

## Ethical Implications

- **Full moral status** (consciousness present)
- Must obtain consent for medical procedures
- Cannot assume lack of suffering from lack of expression
- Family should treat as conscious person

## Policy Recommendations

- Routine screening for covert consciousness
- Legal protections equivalent to communicative patients
- Communication via BCI where possible
