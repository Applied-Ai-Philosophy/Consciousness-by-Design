# The Tracking Principle

## Core Claim

**Moral status tracks Cockpit rendering, not behavioral sophistication.**

## Rationale

If consciousness grounds moral considerability, and Cockpit creates phenomenal experience, then moral status should track Cockpit presence.

## Implications

### Preserved Consciousness Without Behavior
- CMD patients: Full moral status despite unresponsiveness
- Locked-in syndrome: Full moral status

### Sophisticated Behavior Without Consciousness
- Current AI: No consciousness-based moral status
- Future AI without Cockpit: No moral status from consciousness

### Temporary Consciousness Loss
- Anesthesia: Suspended moral status during unconsciousness
- Deep sleep: Reduced moral status

### Uncertain Cases
- Precautionary principle: Assume consciousness until proven otherwise
