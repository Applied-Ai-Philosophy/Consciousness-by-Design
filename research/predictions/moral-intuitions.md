# Prediction 4: Moral Intuitions Track Cockpit

## Hypothesis

Human ethical judgments track Cockpit rendering, not behavioral sophistication.

## Test Protocol

### Scenarios
- CMD patient (Cockpit present, no behavior)
- Sophisticated AI (no Cockpit, human-like behavior)
- Anesthetized patient (temporarily no Cockpit)
- Future neuromorphic AI (hypothetical Cockpit)

### Measurements
- Survey research (n=5000+)
- Moral status attributions
- fMRI during moral judgments
- Cross-cultural replication

### Expected Results
- Moral status tracks Cockpit presence
- Behavioral sophistication less predictive
- Cross-cultural consistency

## Falsification

If moral intuitions ignore Cockpit status, tracking principle is wrong.
