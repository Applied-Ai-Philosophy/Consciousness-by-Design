# Prediction 1: Hyperscanning and Field Coupling

## Hypothesis

Field-coupled conscious systems exhibit inter-brain coherence unexplainable by communication channels alone.

## Test Protocol

### Participants
- Meditation pairs (synchronized states)
- Musical ensembles (coordinated performance)
- Conversational dyads (natural dialogue)
- Joint action tasks (manual coordination)

### Measurement
- Dual EEG/MEG with precise synchronization
- Phase coherence analysis (gamma: 30-80 Hz)
- Information-theoretic bounds on communication
- Control: Same tasks without interaction

### Expected Results
- Coherence exceeding information-theoretic bounds
- Gamma-band phase-locking
- Correlation with subjective reports of connection

## Falsification

If all observed coherence is explainable by classical communication, Field coupling is unnecessary.
